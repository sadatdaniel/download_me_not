getaload

A self-hosted download server with an Android app.

Send it a web link, a video page, a magnet link or a .torrent; it downloads on your own server, and you browse, stream or copy the results from the app, the web panel or the command line. Several people can share one server, each with their own name, PIN and files.

  • Web downloads with many connections, resume and retry
  • Video pages through yt-dlp, with quality, format and subtitles
  • Torrents, including watching while downloading
  • Optional copies to the user's own Google Drive

Source code, installation and documentation: github.com/sadatdaniel/getaload.
